02

Health-status semantics

The layer that makes this a medical companion, not a to-do list. Three first-class voices — never the accent, never each other. A parent reads the colour before the words.

The three voices
Safe · tolerated

Success

Dose taken, watch window clear, no reaction. Sage green — reassuring, not loud.

Mild · watch

Caution

Mild reaction, review pending, watch running. Amber — attention, not alarm.

Severe · emergency

Danger

Reaction plan, 911, severe symptoms. Coral — the loudest voice, reserved for it.

Do & don't

Do

  • Reserve each semantic voice for its meaning — danger is the emergency, never a highlight.
  • Outline primary actions; let the accent live in lines, focus rings and glow.
  • Lean on the ramps for surfaces, borders and muted text — value carries hierarchy.
  • Keep the compact 0.70× spacing; this interface is dense on purpose.

Don't

  • Flood large areas with the accent or any saturated fill.
  • Use pure black or white, or bolden headings past 500.
  • Style a status with the accent, or borrow one voice for another.
  • Stack heavy shadows; elevation is a hairline edge plus ambient darkness.

Not a medical device. This companion app does not diagnose allergy. Clinician instructions always take precedence over anything shown in the app.
